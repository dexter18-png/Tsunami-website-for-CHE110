🌊 Tsunami Risk in India
📌 About the Project

This project is a website created as part of the CHE110 – Environmental Studies course at Lovely Professional University (LPU).

The main focus of this project is Tsunami Risk in India. The website provides information about tsunamis, their causes, the regions of India that are vulnerable to tsunami hazards, their environmental and social impacts, and important safety and preparedness measures.

The project aims to spread awareness about tsunami risks and encourage people to understand the importance of disaster preparedness and environmental safety.

🎯 Objectives

To understand the causes and characteristics of tsunamis.

To identify tsunami-prone regions in India.

To study the environmental, social, and economic impacts of tsunamis.

To highlight the importance of disaster preparedness and risk reduction.

To present environmental studies concepts through an interactive website.

🌏 Tsunami Risk in India

India has a long coastline and several coastal regions that can be affected by tsunami events. The Indian Ocean region, particularly areas along the eastern and western coasts and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, requires awareness and preparedness for tsunami hazards.

The risk of a tsunami depends on factors such as:

Location and magnitude of an undersea earthquake

Seafloor displacement

Coastal geography

Population density in coastal areas

Availability of early-warning systems

Level of disaster preparedness

🚨 Major Impacts of Tsunamis

Tsunamis can cause significant damage to coastal communities and ecosystems, including:

Loss of human life

Destruction of homes and infrastructure

Coastal flooding

Soil and water contamination

Damage to agriculture

Destruction of marine and coastal ecosystems

Economic losses

Displacement of communities

🛡️ Safety and Preparedness

Some important tsunami safety measures include:

Move to higher ground immediately after receiving a tsunami warning or experiencing strong/long-lasting coastal earthquake shaking.

Stay away from beaches, rivers, and low-lying coastal areas.

Follow instructions issued by local authorities.

Keep an emergency kit ready.

Learn evacuation routes and identify nearby safe locations.

Do not return to affected coastal areas until authorities declare them safe.

Spread awareness about tsunami preparedness among family, friends, and the community.
