01 · Understanding tsunamis

A clearer way to understand the ocean's most powerful waves.

A tsunami is a series of long waves produced by a large, sudden displacement of seawater. Most are generated by undersea earthquakes, but landslides and volcanic eruptions can also generate them.

03 · Tsunamis & India

India's coast has a tsunami history — and a warning system.

India's preparedness combines seismic monitoring, sea-level observations, tsunami modelling, a 24×7 warning centre and communication with vulnerable communities.

India's warning system operates around the clock.INCOIS describes a network of seismic stations, tsunami buoys, tide gauges, modelling and a 24×7 warning centre.
2004 changed preparedness in the Indian Ocean.After the 26 December 2004 disaster, India developed the Indian Tsunami Early Warning System at INCOIS.

05 · Preparedness

Know the signs. Know the route.

Preparedness is about recognizing natural warning signs, following authoritative warnings and knowing where to move.

BEFORE

Know where to go

Learn your local evacuation route, identify safer areas and keep emergency supplies accessible.

DURING

Move away from the shore

If you are near the coast and experience strong or long shaking, move inland or to higher ground and follow official instructions.

AFTER

Do not return too soon

A tsunami is a series of waves. Stay away from affected coastal areas until authorities say it is safe.
